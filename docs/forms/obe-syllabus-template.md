## About This File

This is the official university template for an Enhanced OBE (Outcomes-Based Education) syllabus. It comes in **two variants that share the same document code (OVPAA-F-INS-050, Rev. 05, July 11, 2025)**:

- **Main Campus variant**: signatory block as shown under "Signatories (Main Campus)".
- **Satellite Campus variant**: signatory block as shown under "Signatories (Satellite Campus)".

Pages 1 to 5 (university statements, course information, Learning Plan, References, Grading System, Policies, Revision History) are **identical in both variants**. **Only the signatories block on page 6 differs.** Ask the user which variant applies if it is not stated.

A syllabus produced from it is later reviewed using:
- `obe-syllabus-review-checklist.md` (Form 17, OVPAA-F-INS-017)
- `citl-syllabus-review-action-taken.md` (Form 18-1, CITL-F-018)

**Fixed sections** (copy verbatim into every syllabus, do not paraphrase): Mandate, Vision, Mission, Quality Policy, Institutional Outcomes, Core Values, Grading System, Policies, and the fixed rows of the Learning Plan.
**Fill-in sections** (marked `[FILL IN]`): everything specific to the course.

## Document Structure (in order)

1. Title: "Enhanced OBE Syllabus on [course name]"
2. Mandate, Vision, Mission, Quality Policy, Institutional Outcomes, Core Values (page 1, fixed text)
3. Course Information and Course Outcomes (page 2)
4. Learning Plan table (pages 3 to 4)
5. References
6. Grading System
7. Policies
8. Revision History
9. Signatories (Prepared, Reviewed, Approved, Revised); use the Main Campus or Satellite Campus block

## House Style (from Form 17 and the CITL guide)

- Paper: A4, landscape; margins 1 inch on all sides; single line spacing
- Font: Book Antiqua
- Headings: 12 pt; main text: 11 pt (page 1 only: 9 pt)
- References follow APA 7th edition; at least 5 references and 10 e-books/online resources
- Every header page carries the university header, and every page carries the footer line:
  `Document Code: OVPAA-F-INS-050 | Revision no. 05 | Issue Date: July 11, 2025 | Page X of Y`

### Policies

With the implementation of Hybrid Flexible (HyFlex) Learning System the following policies will be applied:

- Hybrid Flexible (HyFlex) Learning System is a combination of face-to-face (onsite) and flexible (remote online and offline).
- The BukSU-HyFlex model requires students in every program to participate in in-person teaching-learning activities (TLAs) that require the facilitation of teachers, return demonstration, and use of laboratories and/or workshop equipment.
- Synchronous online learning is a real-time learning method using chat and video conferencing platforms as virtual classrooms. Synchronous online TLAs are particularly used for students with digital connectivity utilizing tools such as BukSULearn, Google Meet, Zoom, Facebook Messenger, and video apps such as Youtube, and FB Live.
- Asynchronous online learning delivers coursework via email and message board where interaction between teacher and student is delayed. Completing coursework happens in the students' flexible time frame. Posted lecture materials, self-guided e-modules, assignments, video content, and presentations to be read/viewed outside of class via LMS like BukSULearn, google classrooms, Facebook pages or group chats, institutional e-mails, and/or exchanges across discussion boards or social media platforms are some of the methods of asynchronous learning.
- Modular instruction involves the use of learning modules which can be online interactive modules or printed self-instructional modules.
- BukSU employs alternative assessment methods involving various kinds of performance, authentic, and portfolio tasks to build up a multidimensional picture of learner performance, in addition to the conventional tests.
- Academic integrity shall be observed at all times in the performance of all tasks.
- Ethical use of AI shall include verifying information, checking grammar and language structure, checking similarity, generating an outline for academic work, and proper citation.
- Consultation hours will be rendered equivalent to 20% of the instructional time and it can be conducted face-to-face and through online platforms like Messenger and BukSULearn.

## Instructions for Claude (when using this file)

### Creating a syllabus
1. Copy all **fixed sections** verbatim (Mandate through Core Values, Grading System, Policies, fixed Learning Plan rows). Do not rewrite or summarize them; Form 17 item B1 requires them to be stated accurately.
2. Ask the user for the course details (code, title, credit, nominal duration, prerequisite, description) if not provided. Do not invent them.
3. Write Course Outcomes (CO1, CO2, ...) so that:
   - each begins with an active behavioral verb from Bloom's Taxonomy (e.g., demonstrate, analyze, evaluate, formulate);
   - COs are arranged from lowest to highest complexity;
   - the last CO is the terminal/overarching outcome that the Major Output (40% of grade) corresponds to.
4. In the Learning Plan, every row must connect a CO to one or more Specific Learning Outcomes (SLOs), a topic, an innovative approach/strategy, ICT tools (Lec and/or Lab), required readings, an assessment, and Teacher's Notes.
   - SLOs must be precise and observable, and increase in complexity: foundational, then mediating, then terminal/ultimate.
   - Assessments must be performance-based and aligned with the COs and SLOs.
   - Keep the fixed rows for Weeks 1, 9, 18 and 19 exactly as shown.
5. Build the References list in APA 7th edition with at least 5 references and 10 e-books/online resources. Only list real, verifiable sources; if unsure, ask the user rather than inventing citations.
6. Where relevant, mainstream the themes in Form 17 item C3 (innovative and ethical leadership, cultural and natural heritage, gender and development, DRRM and climate change, futures thinking, entrepreneurial and green mindset, or SDGs).
7. Leave signature lines and dates blank; they are for the human signatories.
8. Use the signatory block that matches the campus variant (Main or Satellite). Do not mix roles between them: for Satellite Campuses the second reviewer is the Academic Services Head for Satellite Campuses, not the Dean.
9. When reviewing a Satellite Campus syllabus, note that the review files (Form 17 item H3, CITL item E.2) list the Dean as a signatory. The Satellite template replaces the Dean with the Academic Services Head for Satellite Campuses, so do not mark the syllabus "No" solely for the missing Dean line; mention the difference in Remarks instead and let the human reviewer decide.

### Output as a document
If the user wants a real syllabus file (not markdown), it should be built as a Word document with the House Style above (A4 landscape, 1 inch margins, Book Antiqua, 12 pt headings, 11 pt text with 9 pt on page 1, single spacing) and the footer on every page.

### Self-check before delivering
Run the finished syllabus against `obe-syllabus-review-checklist.md` and `citl-syllabus-review-action-taken.md` and fix any "No" items.
